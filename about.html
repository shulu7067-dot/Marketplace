About Marka

A calmer way to buy and sell locally — homes, vehicles, bikes and more, from people near you.

Why we built this

Most marketplaces bury you in noise — endless filters, listings from the other side of the world, and no real sense of who you're dealing with. Marka is built around the opposite idea: show people what's actually near them, make it easy to search anywhere they choose, and keep the whole experience calm and simple.

What matters to us

  • Local by default. Search any place in the world, set your own radius, or just search near you.
  • Real people. Verified sellers and a straightforward chat make it easy to trade with confidence.
  • No clutter. One clear search, simple filters, and nothing you don't need.
