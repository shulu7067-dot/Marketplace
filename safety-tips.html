Trading safely on Marka

Most trades go smoothly — a little care keeps it that way. Here's what to know before you buy or sell.

Meet in public

Choose a busy, well-lit public place for handovers — a shopping centre or café works well.

Bring someone along

For higher-value items, meet with a friend or let someone know where you're going.

Inspect before you pay

Check the item matches the listing and works as described before any money changes hands.

Look for verified sellers

The verified badge means we've confirmed the seller's identity — an extra layer of trust.

Before you meet

  • Keep the conversation in Marka's chat until you've agreed on the details — it gives you a record if anything goes wrong.
  • Never send payment for something you haven't seen in person, unless you know and trust the seller.
  • Be wary of prices that seem too good to be true, or sellers who rush you into a decision.

If something feels wrong

Trust your instincts. You can block a user from your profile, and if you believe someone is breaking the rules or trying to scam you, tell us — visit contact us and choose "Report a safety concern."
