Terms & privacy

The short version of how Marka works, and how we handle your information. This is a demo build — treat this page as sample content rather than binding legal terms.

Last updated July 2026

Using Marka

Marka connects people who want to buy and sell locally. You're responsible for the accuracy of what you list and for meeting other members safely — see our safety tips. We may remove listings or suspend accounts that break these terms, including scams, prohibited items, or abusive behaviour toward other members.

Location & search

When you search near a place — whether typed in or shared from your device — we use that location only to show you relevant listings and distances for that session. Your device's precise location is never shared with other members; only an approximate area or the distance to a listing is shown when relevant.

Your information

We collect the information you provide when creating a listing, messaging another member, or contacting support. We don't sell your personal information to advertisers. You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account at any time from contact us.

Questions

For anything not covered here, reach out through our contact page and we'll get back to you.
