Help center

Answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Contact us directly.

Buying

  • How do I search a specific area? On the Browse page, tap the Location pill, search any town, city or postcode — or use "Use my current location" — then set a radius.
  • Can I search anywhere in the world? Yes — location search isn't limited to a fixed list of cities; type any place and we'll find it.
  • How do I contact a seller? Open a listing and tap "Message seller" to start a chat.

Selling

  • How do I post a listing? Tap "Post ad" from any page, add photos, a description and your price.
  • Can I boost a listing? Yes — visit the Promote page from your listing to increase its visibility.
  • How do I edit or remove a listing? Go to your profile, find the listing under "Your listings," and choose Edit or Remove.

Account & safety

  • How do I verify my account? Visit verification from your profile to complete the steps.
  • How do I block someone? Open their profile or a chat with them, and choose Block.
  • I'm concerned about a listing or user. See our safety tips, and reach out through contact us.
